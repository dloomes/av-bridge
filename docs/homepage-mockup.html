01 / The estate

Every vendor in your fleet, on one calm dashboard.

Every hospital has a mixed estate. A Poly codec in the boardroom, a Biamp DSP in the MDT suite someone spec'd three years ago, Sony displays in the imaging waiting rooms.

MEDIO Assist speaks to all of them through a growing library of first-class adapters, so your digital team sees one clean view of the whole estate instead of five vendor portals and a spreadsheet.

02 / The night before

Rooms tested overnight. Ready before the first clinic.

Every night we power rooms down, wake them up before your teams arrive, and run the readiness routine your clinical IT lead has defined — dial a test bridge, listen for audio, hang up cleanly.

Rooms that failed are already on our NOC's list by 07:30, cc'd to your service desk, and (on managed-service tiers) an engineer is en route before the AM list starts.

03 / Behind the platform

A 24/7 UK operations team on your side — quietly capable.

Software is only half of Assist. The other half is our people — a UK-based network operations centre monitoring your estate around the clock, spotting the drift before it becomes an outage.

Preventative, not reactive. The point isn't to close tickets faster; it's to raise fewer of them.
