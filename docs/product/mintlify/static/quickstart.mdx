M.A.R.C.U.S. monitors and controls the AV and video collaboration equipment across your estate from one portal. It covers displays, codecs, DSPs, cameras, touch panels and PDUs. A small **Collector** on your network talks to your devices and reports to **M.A.R.C.U.S. Cloud**, which is hosted exclusively in the UK. The Collector needs only one outbound firewall rule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the portal">
    Sign in with your organisation's Microsoft account, or with the email and password your administrator gave you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install a Collector">
    Go to **Collectors** and select **New collector**. Copy the one-line install command it gives you and run it on a Linux or Windows host that can reach your AV network. The Collector shows as online within a minute or so.

    The Collector needs outbound HTTPS (TCP 443) to `*.involvecloud.com` and nothing inbound. See [Network requirements](/deploy/network-requirements).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your devices">
    Go to **Devices** and select **New device**. Choose your Collector, pick the device's **Protocol**, enter its address and credentials, and save. The Collector picks the device up within five minutes, and its status and readings then appear on the device page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get alerted">
    Go to **Notifications** and add an Email, Microsoft Teams or webhook channel. You'll then hear about problems before your users do.
  </Step>
</Steps>
