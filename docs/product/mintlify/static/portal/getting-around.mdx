## Sign in

Open your organisation's M.A.R.C.U.S. address in a browser. How you sign in depends on how your organisation has set things up:

- **Sign in with Microsoft.** If your organisation uses Microsoft Entra ID, select **Sign in with Microsoft** and use your work account. If your organisation requires it, this is the only option shown.
- **Email and password.** Enter your **Work email** and **Password**, then select **Sign in**.

If your organisation has added a support contact, it appears under the sign-in form as **Need help?**

### Forgotten your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot?** next to the password field.
2. Enter your work email and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the link in the email within an hour, and choose a new password of at least 12 characters.

Setting a new password signs you out everywhere else, so a lost or shared session can't carry on.

<Note>
If you sign in with Microsoft, your password is managed by your organisation, not M.A.R.C.U.S. Use your organisation's usual password reset.
</Note>

### If Microsoft sign-in fails

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "The Microsoft account you used isn't in your organisation's Entra tenant." | Sign in with your work account, not a personal or guest account. |
| "We couldn't set up your account." | Contact your M.A.R.C.U.S. administrator. |
| "The sign-in link expired." | Start again from the sign-in page. |

If you can sign in but see almost nothing in the sidebar, your account doesn't have a role yet. Ask your administrator to give you one, or to add your Microsoft group to the sign-in mappings.

## The sidebar

The sidebar on the left takes you everywhere. You only see the items your role allows, so your menu may be shorter than a colleague's.

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Overview**, **Map** | Your estate at a glance. See [Overview and Map](/portal/overview-and-map). |
| **Places** | Your buildings and rooms, with every device and a coloured status dot. Select a building or room to list its devices, or a device to open it. |
| **Monitor** | **Alerts** (with a count of open alerts, red when any is critical), **Collectors**, **Devices**, **Adapters**, **Reports**, **Firmware** and **Activity**. |
| **Manage** | **Locations**, **Assets**, **Room Readiness**, and the administration pages: **Users**, **Roles**, **Sign-in mappings**, **Notifications**, **API tokens** and **Branding**. |
| **System** | **Health** and **Downloads**. |

Select a section heading to collapse or expand it. The portal remembers which sections you've collapsed.

## Choose where you land

When you sign in, you land on **Overview** unless you've chosen **Map**. To change it, open the page you want and select **Set as default** in the page header. That page then shows a **Default** badge.

## Your account menu

Your name, at the top right of each page, opens a menu showing your email address and role, and **Sign out**.

Next to it, the connection indicator shows **Connected** while the portal is receiving live updates. If it shows **Disconnected**, check your network connection; the page reconnects on its own.

<Tip>
Press **Esc** to close any dialog. If you've changed something in a form, the portal asks before discarding it.
</Tip>
