An alert is raised when something needs attention, most often a device or Collector that has stopped responding, or a room that failed its readiness check. The **Alerts** item in the sidebar shows how many are open, and turns red when any is critical.

## What raises an alert

| Alert | Raised when | Clears |
|---|---|---|
| **Device offline** | A device stops responding. Warning by default | Automatically, when it responds again |
| **Collector offline** | A Collector stops checking in. Critical, because none of its devices can be monitored; Warning when a standby Collector has taken over its devices, or when the offline machine is the standby itself | Automatically, when it checks in again |
| **Readiness check failed** | A room's [readiness check](/portal/room-readiness) fails. One alert per room, naming the failed steps. Warning | Automatically, when the room's next check passes |
| **Power cycle failed** | A room's overnight power cycle fails, for example a device doesn't come back on. Warning | Automatically, when the room's next power cycle succeeds |
| Device-specific alerts | A device reports a problem, depending on the device | When resolved |

Alerts are also closed automatically if their device is deleted.

Devices that [Room Readiness](/portal/room-readiness) has switched off for the night don't raise offline alerts until the morning warm-up is over.

## Severity

| Severity | Shown in | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Red | Needs action now: a room or site is affected. |
| **Warning** | Amber | Needs attention soon. |
| **Info** | Blue | For awareness. |

## Work through alerts

**Alerts** has four tabs: **Open** (the default), **Acknowledged**, **Resolved** and **All**. Each alert shows the device, what happened, when it opened and the details.

- Select **Ack** to show you're dealing with it. The alert moves to **Acknowledged**, recording your name and the time, so colleagues don't duplicate the work.
- Select **Resolve** when it's fixed and won't clear on its own.

Offline alerts don't need resolving by hand: they clear the moment the device or Collector is back.

You need the **Acknowledge alerts** or **Resolve alerts** permission for these actions.

## Get alerts outside the portal

Alerts can also be sent by email, to a Microsoft Teams channel, to any system that accepts a webhook, or straight into ServiceNow as incidents. An administrator sets these up; see [Notifications](/admin/notifications).

Notifications go out when an alert first opens, not every time it repeats, so an unstable device doesn't flood your inbox.

## How long alerts are kept

Resolved alerts are kept for 12 months after they're resolved, then deleted. Open and acknowledged alerts are kept until they're resolved.
