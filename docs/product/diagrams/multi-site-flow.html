Collector sizing — practical guidance

Host sizeComfortableStretch (relaxed poll rates)
2 vCPU · 4 GB RAM250 – 500 devicesup to ~1,000 devices
4 vCPU · 8 GB RAM500 – 1,000 devicesup to ~2,000 devices
Heavy codec mix~60% of the above~75% of the above

Capacity depends on poll rate, WAN latency to each site and the adapter mix. Involve confirms sizing for a specific estate during onboarding.

Add a second Collector when one host runs more than ~500 devices, when a remote site is more than 50 ms away and has dozens of devices, or when you want isolation between business units.

What holds this together

  • One Collector, one HTTPS session: one firewall rule, and one host to patch and monitor.
  • HMAC-signed Collector-to-cloud requests, with identity verified on every request.
  • Site traffic stays on your WAN in RFC 1918 space. No AV device is exposed to the internet.
  • Entra ID SSO for every portal user, with per-tenant access and MFA set by your policy.
  • Scoped, revocable API tokens for downstream integrations.
  • UK-hosted exclusively: all data is stored and processed in AWS London (eu-west-2).
  • Offline-aware: if the Collector loses its connection, every device behind it is shown as Collector offline rather than as a stale status.
